Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Eligibility for Returns

You may request a return if:

  • The product received is damaged during transit
  • The product does not match the description or specifications
  • The product is defective or not as advertised

2. Return Window

Returns must be initiated within 7 days of delivery. After this period, we may not be able to process return requests.

3. How to Request a Return

To initiate a return, please contact us with:

  • Your order ID
  • Reason for the return
  • Photos of the product (if applicable)

Contact us via phone (01829775552), WhatsApp, or email (shohrahuddinsowrov2026@gmail.com).

4. Refund Process

  • Refunds are processed within 7-10 business days after return approval
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error

5. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Products that have been opened, used, or altered
  • Products without original packaging (where applicable)
  • Products damaged due to misuse or improper storage

6. Exchange Policy

If you received the wrong product, we will arrange an exchange at no additional cost to you.
